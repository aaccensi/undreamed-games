About

Founded in 2018, Undreamed Games is an indie studio based in the Netherlands.

We make retro-looking games inspired by old-school pixel games and the most memorable action movies of the '80s. Our debut title is Mega City Force.

We tracked, reduced and offset our emissions while developing Mega City Force, offsetting twice our footprint with verified carbon credits.
