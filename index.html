Hello, Agent. Mega City needs your services. Are you ready to uphold the law?

Crime is out of control in Mega City. A corrupt megacorporation squeezes the life out of the citizens, and only the incorruptible agents of the Mega City Task Force stand in its way. Choose your agent and dive into hardcore action against criminals and brutal bosses, set to the electrifying synthwave of Droid Bishop, Timecop1983, Sublunar and more.

Hardcore action

Shoot, punch and use abilities across procedurally generated levels. Loot weapon crates and spend your cash on gear and special contracts.

Pick your agent

Start as a Rookie, a tanky Cyborg or a sneaky Scout, then keep unlocking agents for a total of 12, each with unique abilities and playstyles.

Boss mixtapes

Beat bosses to collect cassette tracks by real synthwave artists, then make your playlist on the boombox back at Headquarters.

Replay in style

Finish the game to unlock harder difficulties, better starting loadouts and instantly recognizable legendary outfits.

About

Founded in 2018, Undreamed Games is an indie studio based in the Netherlands.

A one-person studio making games that are easy to pick up and hard to put down. Its debut, Mega City Force, is a love letter to old-school pixel games and the most memorable action movies of the '80s.

Emissions from developing Mega City Force were tracked, reduced and offset twice over with verified carbon credits.
